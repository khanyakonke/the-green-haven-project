Our Programmes

Discover the initiatives that make our community greener and stronger.

Community Garden

Description: Our community garden provides a space for residents to grow their own fresh produce, learn about sustainable farming, and build connections with neighbours.

Activities: Seasonal planting and harvesting, Gardening workshops, Composting demonstrations, Community workdays

When: Open daily, sunrise to sunset

Location: 161 Jacoba Street, Albemarle, Germiston

Farmers' Market

Description: Every Saturday, our farmers' market brings fresh, locally-grown produce to the community. Support local farmers and artisans while enjoying a vibrant community atmosphere.

What You'll Find: Fresh organic vegetables and fruits, Homemade breads and pastries, Artisanal crafts, Live music and entertainment

When: Every Saturday, 8:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Location: 161 Jacoba Street, Albemarle, Germiston
