Our Services

We offer a variety of services to support our community and promote sustainability.

Community Gardening

Description: We provide garden plots, tools, seeds, and expert guidance for community members to grow their own food.

What's Included: Raised garden bed rental, Access to gardening tools, Seasonal seeds and seedlings, Expert gardening advice, Water supply and irrigation

Cost: Free for members | R50 per month for non-members

Location: 161 Jacoba Street, Albemarle, Germiston

Fresh Produce Market

Description: Our weekly market connects local farmers and artisans with the community, offering fresh, affordable produce.

What's Available: Fresh organic vegetables and fruits, Homemade breads and pastries, Artisanal crafts, Free cooking demonstrations, Kids' activities

When: Every Saturday, 8:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Location: 161 Jacoba Street, Albemarle, Germiston

Educational Workshops

Description: We offer hands-on workshops to teach sustainable living skills to people of all ages.

Workshop Topics: Composting and soil health, Urban gardening basics, Seed saving and propagation, Water conservation techniques, Kids' nature education

Cost: Free for members | R50 per session for non-members

Location: 161 Jacoba Street, Albemarle, Germiston

View All Workshops →

Community Composting Programme

Description: We collect food scraps and garden waste from community members and transform it into nutrient-rich compost for the garden.

How It Works: Drop off your food scraps at our collection point, We process the waste in our industrial composting system, Compost is used in the community garden, Members receive free compost for their own gardens

Cost: Free for members | R20 per bag for non-members

Location: 161 Jacoba Street, Albemarle, Germiston

Volunteer Placement

Description: We connect passionate individuals with meaningful volunteer opportunities in the community.

Volunteer Roles: Garden maintenance and planting, Event setup and coordination, Social media and content creation, Administrative support, Workshop facilitation

Benefits: Gain experience, meet new people, make a difference
