About The Green Haven Project

Our journey, our values, and the people behind it.

From a Vacant Lot to a Thriving Community

The Green Haven Project was born in 2018 when a group of neighbours saw potential in an underused urban space. What started with a few shovels and a dream has grown into a vibrant community hub with gardens, markets, and educational programmes.

Over the years, the organisation has expanded to include weekly farmers' markets, environmental education workshops for all ages, and a community composting programme. The organisation is run by a small staff and a large network of dedicated volunteers, relying heavily on community support and grants to operate.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

MISSION
To cultivate a sustainable and resilient community by providing accessible green spaces, education, and resources that empower individuals to lead environmentally conscious lives.

VISION
A future where every community has access to green spaces, fresh food, and the knowledge to protect our planet for generations to come.

CORE VALUES
Sustainability | Community | Education | Inclusivity | Integrity
